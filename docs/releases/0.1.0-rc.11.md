# Music Index Studio 0.1.0-rc.11

This candidate closes the gaps of the People test of 0.1.0-rc.10 on 2026-11-02 ([ADR 0111](../adr/0111-the-page-of-a-person-checks-the-feeds.md) and [ADR 0112](../adr/0112-a-credit-role-comes-from-a-list.md)). It comes after 0.1.0-rc.10.

## What changed

### Check and correct the feeds of a person

- "Check your feeds" replaces "Where used" on the page of a person. Studio compares each feed with the person, and puts the feed in one of three groups: "Needs a correction", "Not your default address" or "Correct".
- A credit with the name of the person counts as a credit of the person, also with an incorrect key. Thus Studio finds a credit with the key of a different account.
- The check finds these differences:
  - a credit with no Nostr key, or with a key that is not a key of the person
  - a credit with a picture or an information address that is not the value of the person
  - a credit with no role
  - a payment that pays the wallet of the person, but not with the default address
  - a payee with the name of the person that pays a different wallet
- The check uses only the data of your collection. It makes no request to a relay or to a wallet.
- "Correct N Feeds" opens one sheet. You select the changes: "Nostr key", "Payment address", "Picture and information address" and "Role". "Payees with this name that pay a different wallet" starts not selected, because it changes who gets the payment.
- Each feed in the sheet lists what the selected changes correct. "Review in N Feeds" opens the usual review. A credit that has a role keeps its role.

### The page of a person

- The page shows your edits first. "Change your feeds" is lower, and has one row with one line of text for each operation: Use This Address, Rename, Add to All Releases and Remove from Feeds.
- A field saves when you leave it, and then shows "Saved".
- "Member of" is in the section "Artist or label". It shows the role of each feed, with the hint "Studio changes no feed." The header shows the artist or label with its type dot.

### Suggestions and payees

- A suggestion opens one sheet, "Check: {name}". It replaces "Make a Person" and "Add to a Person". The menu "Person" starts on your person with the same name, so you can add items directly, with no new person first.
- The page of a payee with no person has "Add to a Person". "Add a Wallet" on the page of a person lists the payees with no person first.

### Credit roles

- The role of a credit is a menu. The Music group of MSP 2.0 is first: Band, Vocalist, Guitarist, Bassist, Drummer, Keyboardist and Musician. Then come the 11 groups of the Podcast Taxonomy Project, then "Other role…".
- Studio writes the group of the role that you select, in lowercase, for example `role="vocalist" group="music"`. A credit that you do not change keeps its text.
- "Other role…" gives a text field and a group menu.
- The usual role of a person uses the same menu. "Pick from your people" and "Add to All Releases" write the usual role and its group.

### Corrections

- After a merge, Back to the merged person opens the person that kept the items. After "Delete Person", Back opens People.
- The unlock sheet has the email of the account in a hidden username field. Thus a password manager, such as KeePassXC, can find its entry and fill the passphrase.

### Documents

- A new user guide page, [People](../user-guide/people.md), explains the suggestions, the page of a person, "Check your feeds" and "Correct N Feeds".

## What to test

1. Unlock Studio with KeePassXC after the first sign-in. Make sure that KeePassXC fills the passphrase. If it does not, report if KeePassXC shows its icon in the field.
2. Merge two people, then select Back two times. Make sure that Studio shows no error.
3. Open HeyCitizen, then "Check your feeds". Make sure that each credit with the key of City Radio is in "Needs a correction".
4. Select "Correct N Feeds". Keep "Nostr key" and "Payment address", then review and write.
5. Open "Check your feeds" again. Make sure that each feed is correct, and that each payment uses the node address.
6. Open a suggestion with the name of a person. Make sure that the sheet starts on that person, and add the items.
7. Open a payee with no person, and add it to a person with "Add to a Person".
8. Set the role "Vocalist" on a credit and write the feed. Make sure that the feed on the file host has `role="vocalist" group="music"`.

## Known gaps

- The KeePassXC correction follows the guidance of Chromium for a step that asks only for a password. No person tested it with KeePassXC before this candidate.
- A row in "Check your feeds" shows the findings, but not the role of the credit before them.
- No test wrote a correction to a file host before this candidate. The visual check used a staged collection.
- The human tests of Phase 12 (the server shell), of tasks 065 to 075, and of 0.1.0-rc.10 stay open.
