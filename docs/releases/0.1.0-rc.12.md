# Music Index Studio 0.1.0-rc.12

This candidate helps you fill in the details of a person ([ADR 0113](../adr/0113-the-page-of-a-person-suggests-a-picture-and-an-information-address.md)), and lets you dismiss a suggested Lightning address. It also corrects the editor header on a phone. It comes after 0.1.0-rc.11.

## What changed

### Suggestions for the picture and the information address

- Below an empty "Picture address" or "Information address" on the page of a person, Studio lists the values that it found, each with its source and "Use".
- The sources are:
  - the `picture` and the `website` of the Nostr profile of each key of the person
  - the picture and the information address of the credits of the person in your feeds
  - the image and the link of the artist or label feed in "Member of"
- A picture row shows a preview. Equal values from two sources show as one row that names the two sources.
- Below a filled field, one collapsed line, for example "2 other values in your feeds and profiles", opens the same list.
- "Use" puts the value in the field and saves it. The field then shows "Saved". No feed changes. To copy the value to your credits, use "Check your feeds", then "Correct N Feeds" with "Picture and information address".

### Dismiss a suggested Lightning address

- A Lightning address from the Nostr profile of a key of the person has "Dismiss" adjacent to "Add to Wallet" ([ADR 0098](../adr/0098-the-people-module.md) item 18).
- Studio remembers the dismissal for that person, and the address does not come back. A merge keeps the dismissal. Studio sends no keysend lookup for a dismissed address.
- The two buttons are in a line below the address, so on a phone the address has the full width of the row.
- When the key is not the key of the person, remove the key in "Credits". Then its address goes away too.

### The editor header on a phone

- At 760 px or less, "Read Again" has its own row below the title. Before, it took half of the row, the title broke in its words, and the feed address was cut short.
- A title breaks in a word only when the word is wider than the line.

## What to test

1. Open HeyCitizen. Clear "Picture address" and leave the field. Make sure that the list shows the picture of your Nostr profile first.
2. Select "Use" on that picture. Make sure that the field shows "Saved", and that the list closes to one line.
3. Open the collapsed line below "Information address". Select "Use" on an address from your feeds.
4. Open "Check your feeds" and correct the picture and the information address of your credits.
5. If a person shows a Lightning address from an incorrect profile, select "Dismiss". Reload the page and make sure that the address does not come back.
6. On a phone, open an album with a long title and the artist feed. Make sure that the title breaks only between words, and that "Read Again" is below it.

## Known gaps

- A suggestion of a picture or an address has no "Dismiss". When the field has a value, the list stays closed.
- The profile lookups of the suggestions can make the page load slower, by a maximum of the time limit of one profile lookup.
- The human tests of Phase 12 (the server shell), of tasks 065 to 076, and of 0.1.0-rc.10 and 0.1.0-rc.11 stay open.
