# Music Index Studio 0.1.0-rc.24

This candidate makes the corrections from the walk of rc.23 on 2026-11-15. Studio shows the state of a podping after a write, and a fee gets its share in the total of all shares. In a narrow window, the musicindex mark comes first in the bar and opens the root page. Each file picker lets you select an MP3 file.

**Before the first start of this candidate, make a copy of your data folder.** This candidate adds database schema version 43. Rc.23 and older cannot open data that this candidate opened. They show "the collection has schema version 43, and this Studio knows only 42".

## What changed

### Files

- **Fix:** each file picker lets you select an MP3 file. Before, some file pickers showed each MP3 file gray in **Choose Music and Image Files** and in the audio field of a track.

### Releases

- **New:** each row and each tile of the releases list shows the track count after the kind label.
- **New:** the release row above the track list shows the state mark of its feed. The mark leads to **Read Again**.
- **Change:** the menu "Kind" is on the first tab, between "Title" and "Artist". The tab **Settings** has no menu "Kind". A change saves immediately, and the hint says so.
- **Fix:** with a mouse at 390 px, the heading "Releases" stays on its own line. Before, the sort menu covered its text.

### Review and the write

- **Change:** the view bar of Review is a segmented control with two segments, instead of a select menu.
- **Change:** a change of the switch **Hide from apps** shows one row with "Yes" or "No", instead of the rows of its two tags.
- **New:** the row of an added or a removed live item gives the state, the start and the end in local time.
- **New:** after a write with a podping, the feed page shows the state of the podping. Studio asks Music Index each 20 seconds, for a maximum of 15 requests and for 5 minutes after the write.
- **New:** the notice becomes a confirmation when Music Index has read the write. With no read, the notice says so and shows **Check Again**.
- **Change:** the check of a change from outside Studio compares the build date of the index with the build date of your last write. Studio keeps the time and the build date of each write.

### Payments

- **Change:** a fee gets its share in the total of all shares. Each recipient gets its shares divided by the total, also a fee recipient. The shares 100 and 100 and a fee of 10 show 47.6 %, 47.6 % and 4.8 %.
- **Change:** the value page has one label "Split (shares)" and the check box "Fee". A line names each fee and says that it comes out of the payment.
- **Change:** a node address shows the first 10 characters, "…" and the last 8 characters. The hover text and **Copy** give the full address. An npub has the same short form.
- **Change:** a recipient address has the menu of an address row, with **Change**, **Copy** and **Add to People**. **Remove** stays below the row.
- **Fix:** at 760 px or less, the address of a recipient goes on a second line below the name. Before, the address had no space at 390 px.

### The narrow window and the keyboard

- **Change:** in a narrow window, each page other than the root page starts its bar with the musicindex mark. Back, Forward with a mouse, and the title follow it.
- **New:** the mark and the title open the root page with one touch or one click.
- **Change:** the short bar at the left edge of rc.23 is removed.
- **New:** in the grid of tiles, Up and Down go to the same column of the next line, or to its last tile.
- **Fix:** in the Dark theme, the focus ring of a primary button shows 2 px outside the button. Before, it had the color of the button.

## What to test

The tests and the screenshots of each change passed. These steps need the real desktop app, a real file host, a real device or a deploy.

### The upgrade of your data

1. Make a copy of your data folder.
2. Start the AppImage of this candidate. Make sure that your feeds, your people and your vault stay.

### Files

3. On `studio.musicindex.org`, open a new track and select **Choose Music and Image Files**. Make sure that the MP3 files are not gray and that you can select one.
4. Do step 3 again in the AppImage.

### The write and Music Index

5. Write a real feed with **Send a podping** on. Make sure that the notice shows, and that it becomes a confirmation after Music Index reads the feed.
6. Open a real feed with a fee. Make sure that each percentage agrees with the rule "shares divided by the total of all shares".

### The iPhone

7. Open a release and a part of it. Make sure that the mark shows at the top left, before Back. Touch the mark. The root page "Studio" must show immediately.
8. Open a part again. Touch the title. The root page must show.
9. On the root page, swipe from the left edge. The browser must go out of Studio.
10. Touch Back, then swipe forward. Tell us if Forward does not move.

### The keyboard

11. At a narrow width, press Tab from the top of a page. The focus must go to the mark, then Back, then Forward, then the title. Press Enter on the mark. The root page must show.
12. On the releases page, move in the grid with the arrow keys. Up and Down must keep the column.

### Steps that stay open from earlier candidates

13. The steps of "What to test" in the notes of rc.23 stay open.
14. `docs/reviews/2026-11-14-open-older-human-tests.md` has the open tests of Phase 12 and of tasks 049 to 093.

## Known gaps

- The upgrade to schema version 43 ran in tests, not on real data and not with the AppImage.
- The screenshot walk opens 36 of the 58 sheets. The other 22 need walk data that is not available yet.
- In the screenshot drive, Forward did not move after a Back in a narrow window. The cause is not known. Step 10 examines it on a phone.
- The review sheet scrolls slowly in the desktop app with a long draft. The cause is not known.
- A podping for a write of only deletions has no decision.
- Studio does not show the version of the server in its pages.
- The README gates of task 124 stay open.
- The macOS and Windows installers need a tester with that system.
