# Music Index Studio 0.1.0-rc.9

This candidate adds the People module ([ADR 0098](../adr/0098-the-people-module.md) and [ADR 0099](../adr/0099-the-people-destination-and-the-page-of-a-person.md)). It comes after 0.1.0-rc.8.

## What changed

- People replaces Recipients at the bottom of the left panel. The People page shows the changes to write, the suggestions from your feeds, your people, and the payees with no person.
- Studio suggests a person when the same name or the same Nostr key is in the credits and in the value blocks of your feeds. "Make a Person" lists each credit, payment address and Nostr key with a check box. Studio makes no link without your selection.
- Your saved recipients are now people. Each saved recipient became one person with its wallet.
- The page of a person shows the profile, "Where used", the payment addresses, the credits and the support links. You can add a wallet, a Nostr key, a credit name or a support link.
- Studio suggests the Lightning address of the Nostr profile of a person, when the keysend lookup of that address finds the wallet.
- Four operations change many feeds in one step: Use This Address, Rename, Add to All Releases and Remove. Each sheet lists the feeds with check boxes, and its button reads "Review in N Feeds". Add to All Releases shows its result before the review.
- A payee with no person keeps its page, and its operations also use sheets that list the feeds.
- The review of the changes keeps "Write N Feeds" and "Discard" at the top.
- The words are simpler: "Lightning Node", "Lightning Address", "Account", and "changes" in the place of "change set". A fee payee shows "· fee".
- The recipient picker of the value editor shows your people first. The credits editor and the support links editor get "Pick from your people".
- "Export People" gives a JSON file of your people, with the evidence of each link.

## What to test

1. Open People. Check a suggestion, select its items, and select "Make the Person".
2. Open the person. Add a second wallet, a Nostr key and a support link.
3. Use each sheet one time: Use This Address, Rename, Add to All Releases and Remove. Write one change, and discard one change.
4. For a person with a Fountain wallet and a second wallet, use "Use This Address" to move one feed to the second wallet. Then examine the feed on the file host: it has the expected recipient and the expected method.
5. In the value editor, add a recipient from "Pick from your people". In the credits editor and in the support links editor, add an entry from "Pick from your people".
6. On a phone, open People and the page of a person.

## Known gaps

- The human tests of Phase 12 (the server shell) are still open.
- On the page of a person, a wallet that no feed uses shows its own key, with no provider name.
- A rename that Studio wrote shows only the new name in the review line, because the change keeps no old name.
