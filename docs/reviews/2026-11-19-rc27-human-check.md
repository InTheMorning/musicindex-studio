## Findings

1. The dialog of "Choose Folder…" starts at the home folder also when the field has a typed folder. ADR 0223 item 10 gives this start folder. Operator choice, from a drawing: the dialog starts at the typed folder when it exists, else at its nearest existing parent folder, else at the home folder. This changes ADR 0223 item 10, so it needs a decision record. [ADR 0226](../adr/0226-the-typed-start-folder-the-program-name-the-root-order-and-person-tabs.md) items 1 to 4 give the rule. Task 191 is commit b61bfc4. API tests examine each start folder. Open: the human check in the desktop app.
2. Defect: the row "Nostr profile" grew from 44 px to 52 px when the profile picture came. Operator choice: correct it. Now a key row keeps a 32 px place for the picture while it loads. The row "Artwork" does the same. Walk `.studio-dev/journeys/v225b.json` (mode `base`): the row with a key is 52 px before the profile comes.
3. The first tab of a release showed the kind, for example "Single", and its first section had the heading "Album". [ADR 0225](../adr/0225-the-first-tab-of-a-release-is-release.md) gives the tab "Release" and the heading of the kind. Walks `.studio-dev/journeys/v225a.json` and `v225b.json` (mode `base`).
4. Open: the operator does not like the position and the look of the check box "Send a podping" and its hint in the footer of Review. The check box is in the center of the footer, above "Discard the Draft" and "Write to the File Host". Operator choice, from a drawing: the check box and its hint are at the right, directly above "Write to the File Host". [ADR 0205](../adr/0205-publisher-feeds-announcements-and-show-grants.md) already gives this position, so the center position is a defect. Cause: the footer row was at the right, but the long hint made its block almost as wide as the footer. The check box was at the left edge of that block. Correction: the check box and the hint end at the right edge. Walks `.studio-dev/walk/f4-1280.json` and `f4-390.json` with `setup-155b.py`.
5. Defect: in the desktop app, a click on "Open on musicindex.org" does nothing. The context menu item "Open Link" opens the page in the browser. Cause: `tauri_plugin_opener::init()` adds a page script. On a click on a link with `target="_blank"`, the script stops the click and calls the Tauri command `plugin:opener|open_url`. The window has no command (ADR 0124 item 2), so the call failed with no message. The context menu does not use the script. Correction: the app builds the plugin with `open_js_links_on_click(false)`. Then `on_new_window` gets the link, and `link_action` opens it outside (ADR 0124 item 5). Guard: the test `the_opener_plugin_adds_no_link_script` in `crates/desktop-app/src/main.rs`. Check: the release binary on a private Xvfb with the real data copy and a `xdg-open` that writes a log. Before the change, a left click on "Open on musicindex.org" and on the feed address opened nothing. After the change, each click gave its address to `xdg-open`, and the window stayed on the release page.
6. The installed program of the Arch package is `studio-desktop-app`. The operator wants the name `musicindex-studio-desktop`. [ADR 0226](../adr/0226-the-typed-start-folder-the-program-name-the-root-order-and-person-tabs.md) items 5 and 6 give the name. Task 192 is commit 0143817. The release binary on Xvfb has the window class `musicindex-studio-desktop`. Open: the operator installs the Arch package and starts the app from the menu.
7. Defect: after a write of a listed release with **Send a podping** on, the notice "You wrote this feed at {time}…" showed. After the time limit, the notice disappeared. The confirmation "Music Index has your write of {time}." did not show, and the text "Music Index has not read your write…" did not show. The app was the Arch package of `8425d74`. Cause: the write of the test album has the build date 1795112661, and the podping went at 1795112695. Music Index gives the feed `created_at` 1795112867, so the index held the feed only 172 seconds after the podping. Before that time, the route gave `index_read` null. `writeNotice` gave no notice for null after the podping, and `nextNoticeLoad` stopped the reads. Thus the confirmation could not come. Correction: after a podping, `index_read` null is a write that Music Index did not read (ADR 0175 item 1). The notice stays, the reads continue to the limit, and then the notice gives **Check Again**. A write with no podping gives no notice, as before. Test: `crates/api/ui-tests/outsidecheck.test.mjs`.
8. On the tab **Value Routes**, the check box **Fee** of an open recipient moves the recipient to the section "Fees", below **Add a recipient**. When the operator clears **Fee**, the recipient goes back and closes. The row moves away from the pointer. After the check, the other recipient (HeyCitizenNode) was open, and the fee recipient was closed. Cause: the group of a row followed the check box at once, but [ADR 0144](../adr/0144-recipients-use-one-picker-and-sort-by-split.md) item 4 says that a row does not move while the focus is in it. Also, the two groups were two lists, so a moved row got a new element. Correction: a row changes group only at the next sort, when the focus leaves the row. The rows and the headings are one keyed list, so that a moved row stays open. Walk `.studio-dev/journeys/vfee-a.json` (mode `base`). The walk page has no window focus, so the walk sends the `focusout` event itself.
9. Defect: in the sheet **Add a Recipient** of the desktop app, the type "LNURL Address" shows a very large "@" icon behind the hint and the field. The icon fills the width of the sheet. The app was the Arch package of `8425d74` (WebKitGTK). The browser screenshots of the walks did not show it. The cause is not examined yet. The same icon shows in the sheet **Add a Wallet** of a person page in Safari on the iPhone (WebKit). Cause: the `Icon` helper gives an `svg` element with no size, and `.type-panel > .icon` gave it no size. WebKitGTK drew it at 688 × 688 px. Correction: the default rule of musicindex ADR 0065 R-FIT-7 item 2 in `styles.css`, and 20 px for `.type-panel > .icon`. The walk tool runs `unsizedIcons` at each width. WebKit walk `.studio-dev/journeys/wkicon-a.json` with `.studio-dev/wk-run.sh`: 20 × 20 px after the change.
10. On the iPhone, a forward swipe from the root page always opens the page of the artist. The operator expected the page that was open before the root page: the outline "Sections" of a release. Cause: musicindex ADR 0080 R-TITLE-1 item 4 tells the home button to go back with `history.go(-n)`, and Forward keeps the pages after the root entry. Thus Forward opens the first page after the root page. Operator choice, from a drawing: the home button adds the root page as a new history entry. Back then returns to the page that the person left, and Forward has no page. This changes a rule of musicindex, so the request went to the orchestrator. musicindex commit e0d4407 changes R-TITLE-1 items 4 and 5. Correction: `rootNav` adds the root page after the current entry, `toRoot` gives it the history index of the current entry + 1, and `canBack` is false on each root page. Walk `.studio-dev/walk/f10-390.json` with `setup-none.py`: for the home button and the title button, Forward stays on the root page, and Back opens the page that the person left at the same address.
11. On the tab **Value Routes**, the section "Fees" shows below "＋ Add a recipient". The operator wants "Fees" above "＋ Add a recipient". Operator choice: "＋ Add a recipient" is after the fees and the fee line. Walk `vfee-a`, screenshot `05-add-last-390`.
12. The rows **Settings**, **People** and **Hosts** of the root page are at the top in the narrow layout and at the bottom in the wide layout. The operator does not like this difference. [ADR 0223](../adr/0223-a-split-in-shares-or-percent-a-release-search-and-one-tap-on-a-phone.md) item 7 gives the narrow order. Operator choice, from a drawing: the rows are at the bottom in both layouts, after the feed groups. This changes ADR 0223 item 7, so it needs a decision record. [ADR 0226](../adr/0226-the-typed-start-folder-the-program-name-the-root-order-and-person-tabs.md) items 7 and 8 give the order. Task 193 is commit d5f9b82. Walks `.studio-dev/walk/p193-390.json` and `p194-1280.json` with `setup-194.py`.
13. The person pages have no tabs. The other long pages have tabs. The operator wants tabs on the person pages. Operator choice, from a drawing: four tabs. **Profile** has the linked pages and the suggestions of the check. **Feeds** has "Where this person is", the credits and "More from your feeds". **Payment** has the payment and the support links. **Settings** has "Change your feeds" and the delete. The head stays above the tabs. This needs a decision record. [ADR 0226](../adr/0226-the-typed-start-folder-the-program-name-the-root-order-and-person-tabs.md) items 9 to 12 give the tabs. Task 194 is commit 786ee36. Walks `.studio-dev/walk/p194-1280.json` and `p194-390.json` with `setup-194.py`.
14. Open: the operator wants undo and redo where possible. Now only a merge of two persons has **Undo** ([ADR 0217](../adr/0217-a-new-artist-sees-the-result-of-a-write-the-payment-types-and-an-undo-of-a-merge.md) items 12 to 17). Next: a list of the operations that can have undo and redo, and a proposal for the operator. This is a design change across modules, so it needs a decision record.
15. Defect: in a new Safari tab on the iPhone, a back swipe before a touch opened the root page of Studio. A forward swipe after that did nothing. After a touch, a forward swipe opens the page again (step 15). The cause is not examined. A likely cause (inference): WebKit skips the history entries that a page adds before a user action. Examine it together with finding 10.

## AppImage Build On Arch

The local build of the AppImage needs `patchelf`. Give these variables to `cargo tauri build` in `crates/desktop-app`:

- `GSTREAMER_PLUGINS_DIR` from `tools/release_desktop.py appimage-plugins --source /usr/lib/gstreamer-1.0`
- `GSTREAMER_HELPERS_DIR=/usr/lib/gstreamer-1.0`
- `NO_STRIP=true`
- `APPIMAGE_EXTRACT_AND_RUN=1`
